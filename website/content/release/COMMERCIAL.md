# Yscale Commercial Licensing

This page describes the licensing policy, not an executed commercial license.

The [Yscale Community License](LICENSE) makes personal use and
internal self-hosting free of license fees for organizations of every size.
There is no revenue threshold or mandatory annual fee for internal use.
Cloud-provider charges remain your responsibility.

## When you need a commercial license

- You sell, rent, lease or sublicense Yscale or a modified version, including
  as part of a bundle.
- You provide Yscale, its burst-management functionality or compute capacity
  brokered by it to third parties as a hosted or managed service, whether
  separately charged, bundled or offered free of charge.

Running your own applications with Yscale, including applications used by
paying customers, does not require a commercial license as long as you are
not providing Yscale's functionality or brokered compute capacity to those
customers. Internal development, staging, CI, production and evaluation are
covered by the free internal-use terms.

## Terms

A separate paid written agreement must explicitly grant the resale or hosting
rights you need. Pricing, duration, scope, support and any service commitments
are negotiated in that agreement; this repository sets no default price or
SLA. Contacting the licensor or paying an invoice alone does not grant rights
beyond the executed agreement.

Yscale does not offer its own managed hosting service. A commercial license
grants agreed rights to operate your own offering; it is not a subscription
to a Yscale-operated service and does not include hosting or infrastructure
operation. Any support commitments must be expressly agreed separately.

## One full-source distribution

The fabric factory, tenant lifecycle and quota implementations are
included in the same source distribution as the other components. There is
no separate feature-restricted edition. Their presence does not grant the
right to operate Yscale as a service for third parties without the required
paid license.
